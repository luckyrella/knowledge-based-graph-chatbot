=== PAGE 5 ===
EXISTING SYSTEM
? Existing System: Traditional cloud monitoring systems detect suspicious activities 
and generate alerts for attacks. 
? Technologies/Methods: Rule-based detection, threshold monitoring, logs, network 
monitoring, and authentication monitoring. 
? Limitations: Mostly depend on predefined rules and require manual analysis and 
response. 
? Problems/Challenges: Difficulty in identifying the root cause, delayed response, 
and limited automation. 
? Research Gap: Existing research shows the need for AI-based incident analysis, 
LLM/RAG support, and automated response for faster and more effective attack 
handling.

PROPOSED SYSTEM
? Continuously monitor cloud application activities, network requests, and user 
authentication. 
? Detect DDoS, SQL Injection, Brute Force, and Privilege Escalationattacks. 
? Create an incident and use Multi-Agent AI to analyze the attack and identify its 
possible root cause. 
? Use LLM + RAG to retrieve relevant information and support the response decision. 
? Perform predefined automated actions, verify the result, generate an alert, and 
record the incident report.
