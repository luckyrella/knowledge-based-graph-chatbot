Placement Process

01

Pre-Placement Training

Aptitude, coding, soft skills, and mock interviews from 2nd year onwards.

02

Company Registration

Companies register with T&P cell and share job descriptions and eligibility.

03

Written Test / Online Test

Aptitude, logical reasoning, and technical assessments conducted on-campus.

04

Technical Interview

Domain knowledge, problem-solving, and project discussions with panelists.

05

HR Round & Offer

Final round + offer letter with package details and joining date.

Training Programs

Technical Training

Rigorous training in coding (Python, Java, C++), Data Structures, Algorithms, and core subjects. Industry-level projects and hackathons.

Soft Skills

Communication skills, group discussions, mock interviews, personality development, and presentation skills workshops.

Aptitude Training

Quantitative, logical, and verbal reasoning training by industry experts. Regular tests and performance tracking.

Certification Programs

AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Salesforce, and Oracle certification preparation in collaboration with industry partners.

Industry Internships

Mandatory summer internships and industry projects with partnered companies from 3rd year onwards.

Competitive Coding

Regular coding contests on LeetCode, HackerRank, CodeChef, and internal coding challenges with leaderboards.
