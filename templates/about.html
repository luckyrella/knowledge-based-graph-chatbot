Sphoorthy Engineering College

Established in 2004 by the Thoma Educational Society, Sphoorthy Engineering College (UGC Autonomous) has been a beacon of technical education and innovation for over 22 years (2004–2026).

Affiliated to Osmania University and approved by AICTE, SPHN offers undergraduate B.Tech programs in 7 cutting-edge engineering branches with over 960+ seats, alongside postgraduate M.Tech and MBA programs.

Located in Nadargul Village, Near B.N. Reddy Nagar, Saroornagar Mandal, Hyderabad, Telangana 501510.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be a leading institution imparting quality education and creating competent professionals with technical competence, human values, and social responsibilities to serve society and industry globally.

Our Mission

To provide state-of-the-art infrastructure, qualified faculty, industry partnerships, and holistic development to enable students to excel academically, professionally, and ethically in an ever-evolving world.

Key Highlights

Autonomous Status

Conferred UGC Autonomous status, allowing SPHN to design industry-oriented dynamic curricula, adopt modern evaluation methods, and offer fast-track career learning paths.

NAAC Accreditation

Accredited with Grade A by NAAC, validating our superior academic standards, faculty credentials, research outcome, and campus infrastructure.

Industry Partnerships

Strategic MoUs and collaborations with top tech corporations and industry leaders for internships, hands-on projects, workshops, and placement assurance.

Research & Development

Dedicated R&D center encouraging student innovation, faculty research publications, patents, prototypes, and interdisciplinary technical initiatives.

Smart Classrooms

Modern ICT-enabled interactive classrooms, advanced computing laboratories, and high-speed Wi-Fi network enabling blended digital learning environments.

Hostel & Transport

Comfortable separate hostels for boys and girls with nutritious food, plus a fleet of comfortable buses connecting the campus across all major twin-city routes.
