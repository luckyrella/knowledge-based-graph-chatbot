Join Sphoorthy Engineering College and take the first step towards a brilliant career.

Admission Process

B.Tech Programs

  • Category A (70%): Through TS EAMCET counseling based on merit rank.
  • Category B (30%): Management/NRI quota based on JEE Main / EAMCET / Intermediate marks.

Lateral Entry (B.Tech)

  • 10% of total intake is available for Diploma holders into 2nd year.
  • Admission through TS ECET counseling.

PG Programs

  • MBA: Through TS ICET counseling.
  • M.Tech: Through TS PGECET / GATE scores.

Eligibility & Requirements

Document Checklist

  • SSC & Intermediate (10+2) Marks Memos
  • Entrance Exam Hall Ticket & Rank Card (EAMCET/ICET/ECET)
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) & Bonafide Certificates
  • Caste/Community Certificate (if applicable)
  • Income Certificate (for fee reimbursement)
  • Aadhar Card Copy & Passport Size Photographs
